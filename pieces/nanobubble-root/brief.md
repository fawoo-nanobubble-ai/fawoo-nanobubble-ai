# 산소 나노버블 — 뿌리의 숨 (15s, 9:16, sketchbook)

**Idea:** 큰 기포는 떠나고, 작은 나노버블은 남아서 뿌리에 산소를 준다.
**Device:** the terracotta oxygen nanobubble is the only colour; the root goes from graphite (can't breathe) to ink (breathing).
**Format:** one sketchbook page, camera dive in and back out; no cuts, no morphs. Clock: estimated Korean voice-over (lines in `src/head.html` → `LINES`).
**Sound:** composed score, 120 BPM, D major. Hush 9.75–10.5s, loudest stab + sub at 10.5s, decay under the signature.

## Claims and sources
- Roots respire with dissolved oxygen; in hydroponics it is their main supply; shortage harms roots — BMC Plant Biology 2024 (doi 10.1186/s12870-024-04943-7); Environment Control in Biology 32(2):125 (doi 10.2525/ecb1963.32.125).
- Bulk nanobubbles have negligible buoyancy and have been reported to persist for days to weeks — Nirmalkar, Pacek & Barigou 2018, Langmuir; NJIT fac_pubs/4311. Mechanism still debated: the piece shows only the observation ("머문다").
- Oxygen nanobubble treatment raised dissolved oxygen in hydroponic lettuce — SWU-01 study (ResearchGate 387005317); ISHS Acta Hort. 1377_86.
- Deliberately left out: yield or root-length multiples (single abstract), "first", company DO figures.
