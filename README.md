# FAWOO NANOTECH (화우나노텍) — Nanobubble Technology

FAWOO NANOTECH develops nanobubble generators that form bubbles by surface friction in a flow path,
and applies them to industrial water problems: neutralizing strongly alkaline wastewater with CO₂,
carbon capture and utilization (CCU) in concrete, and oxygen nanobubble water for irrigation and
aquaculture. CES Innovation Awards 2025 and 2026.

## Application areas

| Area | What nanobubbles do |
|---|---|
| Alkaline wastewater neutralization | CO₂ nanobubbles lower high-pH wastewater without acid dosing |
| CCU (carbon capture and utilization) | CO₂ delivered as nanobubbles for mineralization, including concrete |
| Agriculture and aquaculture | Oxygen nanobubble water raises dissolved oxygen in irrigation and fish-farm water |
| Sterilization and cleaning | Ozone nanobubble water |
